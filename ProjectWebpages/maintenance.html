Maintenance schedule

A long-term maintenance library covering routine inspection and service intervals once the M104 turbo swap is on the road. Intervals below are general guidance to start from — confirm against the M104 factory service manual and your specific oil/parts specs, and expect turbo + track use to shorten some of these.

Project hub Parts database Purchase planner

Oil

Turbo engines and track use generally call for shorter intervals than a stock N/A schedule — confirm a specific mileage/time interval against your oil spec and the factory manual rather than assuming stock figures apply.

Transmission

Check fluid condition and service per the transmission actually fitted (see the transmission-match part once decided).

Diff

Inspect for leaks and wear at each major service.

Coolant

Replace at the interval defined by the cooling system parts actually installed — verify with the coolant/hardware manufacturer's spec.

Brake fluid

Refresh on a regular cycle (commonly ~2 years) — more frequent if the car sees track/autocross heat cycling.

Power steering

Retained per the build plan — inspect fluid level and pump noise.

Air filter

Replace when restrictions or contamination are found; check more often once the turbo intake is live.

Valve cover

Inspect for seepage and gasket condition.

Timing chain inspection

Critical check during major service events.

Turbo inspection

Inspect oil feed, wastegate, and boost response — see turbo kit detail.

Suspension / air cups

Inspect Bilstein B8 struts and air-cup hardware for leaks/wear each major service. See detail.

Wheel torque

Re-check Sparco Terra wheel bolt torque after the first heat cycle post-install, then per normal interval.
